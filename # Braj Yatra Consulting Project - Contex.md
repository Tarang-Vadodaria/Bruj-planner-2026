# Braj Yatra Consulting Project - Context Handover (MVP)

> **Project Goal**
>
> Produce a consulting-grade, evidence-backed itinerary for a family Braj Yatra during Raksha Bandhan (27–29 Aug 2026). The report should withstand scrutiny from experienced travellers and local tour operators. Every recommendation should be traceable to evidence, assumptions should be explicit, and all major trade-offs should be discussed.

---

# 1. Background

This started as helping understand a WhatsApp family group where multiple people were suggesting itineraries.

The objective evolved into:

> Design the best possible Braj itinerary for this specific family.

Not "tourist itinerary".

Not "cover maximum places".

But the **best itinerary for this exact group.**

# 21. Post-Consultation Findings (Added July 2026)

## Finding 1: Taj Mahal is closed Fridays

**Taj Mahal is closed every Friday. 28 August 2026 is a Friday.** This definitively resolves the Taj debate. It is not a matter of preference — it is an operating-hours constraint. If the family wants Taj, it must be a separate trip on a non-Friday.

**Source:** Multiple independent sources — tourism.gov.in, agra.nic.in, timings.tajmahal.gov.in — confirmed.

## Finding 2: Traditional Raksha Bandhan morning = Yamuna Snan + Yam Raj Puja

Input #2 (Western Braj Circuit proposal) correctly identified that the traditional Sravana Purnima observance in Braj begins with a holy dip in the Yamuna and a visit to the Dharmaraj (Yam Raj) Temple at Vishram Ghat. This is done for brothers' long life and wellbeing — directly connected to the Raksha Bandhan spirit.

This is a genuine upgrade to the itinerary and has been incorporated into Day 2 morning.
